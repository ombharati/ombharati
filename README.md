### HI, I'M OM

Computer Science · Undergraduate

</div>

I’m interested in **system design**: understanding how different pieces
fit together, how a workflow moves from one step to another, and where
software can make that process simpler.

A lot of that interest leads me to **automation**.

I like the idea of building systems that take care of the repetitive parts
of work, giving people more time and attention for the parts that actually
need them. Not replacing the human, but making the human more capable.

I'm also somewhat old-fashioned about the tools I enjoy. I still have a
soft spot for **Java and C++**, and I'd rather understand a technology
properly than chase whatever happens to be trending.

I'm early in this journey, so I don't consider myself a system designer
in the enterprise sense. I'm learning the fundamentals, experimenting
with ideas, and gradually figuring out what good systems actually look like.

The projects here are fairly simple for now. I'm working on some larger ideas in the background, but I'm taking my time with them. I'd rather understand what I'm building before putting it out here.
